# Solace Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 22, 2026

Solace is a Discord wellness companion operated by SomeoneListens.

## Information Solace processes

Solace processes Discord server, channel, role, and user identifiers when they are needed to provide a requested feature. Server configuration includes the selected channel, timezone, posting time, enabled content, optional notification role, and the identifier of the person who last configured Solace.

Solace also keeps content-rotation history and timestamps for automatic daily posts. Operational logs and health data may contain command names, timestamps, technical errors, and service performance information.

## Information Solace does not collect

- Check-in selections are not saved.
- Journal responses are not requested or saved.
- Private resource selections are not saved.
- Solace does not sell personal information.
- Solace does not use wellness interactions for advertising or user profiling.

## Storage and retention

Server configuration and operational data are stored on infrastructure controlled by SomeoneListens. Configuration remains until it is changed, the bot is removed from the server, or deletion is requested. Temporary logs and monitoring history are retained only as needed to operate and troubleshoot Solace.

## Sharing

Solace uses Discord to receive commands and deliver responses. Infrastructure monitoring may use Telegram for private operator alerts. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or necessary to protect the service and its users.

## Your choices

Server managers can disable automatic posts, remove the notification role, change the configured channel, or remove Solace from their server. Server owners may request deletion of retained server configuration through the official SomeoneListens support channel.

## Children and wellbeing information

Solace provides general wellbeing information and is not a medical provider, therapist, or emergency service. Users should avoid entering personal or sensitive information into Discord commands.

## Changes

This policy may be updated as Solace changes. The date at the top will identify the latest revision.

## Contact

Questions and deletion requests can be submitted through the official SomeoneListens website or support server once those links are published in Solace's application profile.
